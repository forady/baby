Baby,

I just finished the entire movie and it was really nice dyud. Haysss. Kahibaw ka, funny lang because before, whenever I watched romcoms, I’d usually feel a little sad after. I’d watch people fall in love and think, “Hays, kanindot. I want something like that pud.” I could enjoy the movie, pero naa gyud always that little emptiness afterward. Murag happy ka for them, pero at the same time, you’re wondering if you’ll ever get to experience something like that yourself.

But now, I finished one and I’m just… genuinely happy. HAHAHAHAHAHA. Wala na tong feeling nga “I wish I had that.” Instead, I kept thinking about you. And I realized nga maybe it’s because I finally have someone who makes me understand why people love love stories so much. 🥹

You’ve changed so many things for me without even trying, baby. You made love feel less like something I just watch other people experience and more like something I actually get to have. Something real. Something that’s happening to me. And hayyysss, I really love you for that.

I won’t lie, the thought of losing you scares me. I really hope dili dyud ta magbuwag. Commitment has always scared me because there are so many things that could go wrong. You know me—I second-guess almost everything I do. My answers, my decisions, my choices. I’m rarely ever completely sure about anything.

Pero when it comes to you? Weirdly enough, I don’t have to convince myself.

I just know I want you.

And maybe that’s one of the biggest things you’ve given me, baby. You made someone who has always been afraid of being certain brave enough to finally be certain about someone. I don’t know what tomorrow looks like, but I know that right now, with everything that I know and everything that I feel, I want it to be you.

I know there will be days when you’re sad, when your thoughts get heavy, or when you start questioning things again. And I also know I won’t always have the perfect words to comfort you. Tbh, bogo dyud ko ana usahay HAHAHAHAHAHA. Sometimes I wish I could just hug you and somehow transfer everything I feel into you so you’d understand it all without me having to explain anything.

But if I can’t always make the sadness disappear, then at least I can stay with you through it.

I’ll listen to you. I’ll hug you. I’ll annoy you until you smile HAHAHAHAHAHA. And I’ll remind you as many times as you need that you are loved. I don’t expect you to be happy all the time just because you’re with me. Your sad days are part of you too, baby. Your worries, your overthinking, your uncertainties—I don’t want you to feel like you have to hide those parts of yourself from me just to be easier to love.

I want to be there for those days just as much as I want to be there for the good ones.

I never want you to feel lonely while loving me, baby.

And please never think nga kulang imong love towards me. I feel it. I really, really do. I feel it in the little things you do, in the way you talk to me, the way you make time for me, the way you let me into your life, even in the way you worry about us. You make me feel loved more than you probably realize. So if you ever wonder whether you’re giving me enough, you are, baby. More than enough.

Maybe we don’t know where life will take us. Maybe there are still a million uncertainties waiting for us. But I don’t want to spend something this beautiful being scared of an ending that hasn’t even happened. I don’t want to love you while constantly preparing myself to lose you.

I’d rather just love you now. Fully. While I have you.

I know you don’t necessarily need me in your life. You can stand on your own. You had a whole life before I came into it, and I know you’ll always be your own person. But me? I sure as hell know that I want you in mine.

And not just for the big, romantic moments either. I want the ordinary days with you. Movies, dates, random stories that probably don’t matter to anyone else, studying together, eating together, bad moods, teasing each other, laughing at stupid things, sitting beside each other doing absolutely nothing. I want the boring days, the exciting days, the heavy days, and all the little days in between.

Maybe that’s what I’ve been wanting all along whenever I watched those romcoms. Not some perfect movie kind of love, but someone I can share my life with—even the most ordinary parts of it.

And somehow, baby, I found that in you.

You’re probably peacefully sleeping right now while I’m here being dramatic after watching a romcom HAHAHAHAHAHA. But when you wake up and read this, I hope you remember one thing:

You went to sleep loved, and you woke up still loved.
